QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can you quote a fixed price now? +

Custom integrations depend on scope, existing systems, and requirements. We confirm a quote after discovering the actual job.

Do I need AI subscriptions? +

It depends. Local AI may cover some requirements without recurring model API fees, while hosting, telephony, and other services can have separate costs.

Will a bot replace my sales team? +

No. AI can speed up replies, triage, and follow-ups, while humans remain in control of exceptions and important decisions.

Are the courses and books available now? +

Those categories are reserved for future releases. No payment or access is offered until a product is ready.
