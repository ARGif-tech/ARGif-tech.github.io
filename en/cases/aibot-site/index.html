A website AI chat that answers 24/7

Aibot on 777xsz.shop answers visitors in 1–3 seconds around the clock, in three languages, and sends orders to WhatsApp.

The task

A website visitor wants an answer now, not after a manager is free. The owner does not want to store other people's conversations or pay for services nobody needs.

How it works

The chat runs in the cloud and answers around the clock, even when the owner's computer is off. If the cloud model is unavailable, AR App on the owner's computer takes the question. If both are unavailable, the bot suggests WhatsApp.

Speed

Replies arrive in 1–3 seconds. Pricing questions get a pre-approved answer instantly.

Security and privacy

Conversations are not stored on the server: a question is deleted right after it is answered. The chat accepts requests only from its own site, has a message limit, and every reply is checked for links, prices and pretending to be human.
